# World News — Saturday, 21 November 2026

A single-image newspaper front page for the make.fun `news-image-20261121` bounty, summing up the world news of Saturday, 21 November 2026. The front page itself is a 1024×1024 PNG, drawn with real rendered text.

## The six stories

- **Russia's mass attack on Ukraine** — more than 400 drones and 110 missiles fired at Ukraine overnight; at least eight people killed in Kyiv, and two missiles came down in Poland, which its prime minister called a deliberate provocation.
- **Wall Street slides into correction** — the S&P 500 fell 3.7% and the Nasdaq 5.28% on Friday, with chip stocks losing $2.3 trillion in a week.
- **COP31 deadlocked** — climate talks in Antalya ran into overtime as nearly 200 nations split over fossil fuels.
- **FBI and US Department of Agriculture hacked** — both were hit by cyberattacks amid wider outages in the US.
- **Earthquake shakes Peru** — a magnitude 6.9 quake struck the Peruvian Andes and was felt in Lima; no damage was reported at once.
- **Bayer's $7.25 billion settlement** — a US judge validated the Roundup settlement for future patients with non-Hodgkin's lymphoma, with no admission of responsibility.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` into the output directory as `entry.png`.
